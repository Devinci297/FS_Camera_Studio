# FS Camera Studio 0.5.3

**Update now downloads and installs the update for you.** No browser download or setup wizard is needed for future updates.

- The updater remembers your selected simulators and their exact installation folders, verifies the official download, and runs setup silently. Saved camera profiles are preserved.
- If MSFS or X-Plane is running, choose **Restart and install** after saving your views and flight. The updater requests a normal close, waits for exit, installs, and reopens the simulators it closed. It never forcibly terminates a simulator.
- Choose **When I close the sims** to keep flying; installation starts automatically after you close them. Cancel remains available before setup starts.
- Manual installer download is tucked into **Update troubleshooting**, for recovery only.
- Automatic notifications still require your click before an update is installed.

Install this setup once if you are upgrading from an older version: 0.5.0/0.5.1 cannot acquire a new updater without installing it, and 0.5.2 did not record the folders needed for fully silent upgrades.

Automated updater, UI, native and isolated installer checks are recorded in the [verification record](https://github.com/Devinci297/FS_Camera_Studio/blob/main/doc/test-evidence/2026-11-07-v0.5.3.md). Live future-version upgrade acceptance remains pending. In MSFS, the Windows update-link handoff still needs a live check; XPCS launches the helper directly. The Start-menu **Check for updates** shortcut is also available.
