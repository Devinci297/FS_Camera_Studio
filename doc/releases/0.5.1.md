# FS Camera Studio 0.5.1

A maintenance release for Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 and XP Camera Studio for X-Plane 12.

Download **FS-Camera-Studio-0.5.1-Setup.exe**, close the simulators and choose MSFS, X-Plane, or both.
Saved profiles and preferences are preserved. Existing 0.5.0 installations can discover this update through
**Settings > Saving & updates > Check for updates**.

## Changes

- **2,000 cameras per profile**, up from 500. Existing profiles need no migration. Airport generation keeps its
  separate 500-camera budget, leaving room for imported and personal cameras, including Drop drone here.
- **Move, rotate and zoom while watching traffic.** The target stays selected and chase views follow it.
  Mouse look opens traffic-camera controls; Recenter restores framing. Manual adjustment stops Auto-spot
  switching. Saved aircraft and airport camera poses are unchanged.
- **Assign native X-Plane camera keys from XPCS.** Open Assign key binding, explicitly select your native
  controls profile, capture a key, review conflicting commands and choose **Assign after exit**. The bundled
  helper waits until X-Plane closes, backs up the preferences and removes conflicting assignments before
  adding the XPCS command. Changes are effective on the **next X-Plane launch**. Change, Remove, Add another
  key and cancellation of pending changes are supported.
- Larger profiles can cross the panel bridge with bounded 8 MiB messages; individual frames still respect the
  4,000-byte limit. Native frame sizing includes JSON escaping and envelope overhead.

## Notes

Camera shortcut commands remain `xpcs/view_1` through `xpcs/view_30`; this is separate from the saved-camera cap.
Other X-Plane commands and controller buttons are assigned in native Keyboard/Joystick settings. Head tracking
is controlled through X-Plane settings. Windows x64 only; X-Plane 12.4.4 or newer is required.

Automated validation is recorded in the [verification record](https://github.com/Devinci297/FS_Camera_Studio/blob/main/doc/test-evidence/2026-11-07-v0.5.1.md).
Live simulator acceptance of the 0.5.1 changes is pending; the successful live tests of 0.5.0 do not cover this patch.
